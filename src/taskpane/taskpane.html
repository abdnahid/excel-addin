Bangla Converter

Select cells, then convert them in place. Formula cells are left untouched.

Options

Bijoy text is made of English letters, so without this, English cells in the selection would be garbled.

Formulas

=BANGLA.TOANSI(A1): Unicode → Bijoy

=BANGLA.TOUNICODE(A1): Bijoy → Unicode

Both accept a range (e.g. A1:A100) and spill the results.
